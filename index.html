About

I am a senior researcher in computer vision and multimodal learning at Huawei Hisilicon. My work focuses on translating cutting-edge research into commercial AI solutions. I have developed perceptual visual quality enhancement models currently deployed on cloud media services and low-power multimodal perception models that run in agents for smartphones. Before joining Huawei, I earned a dual PhD in Computer Science from City University of Hong Kong and Dalian University of Technology in 2023. My research has been published at top-tier venues including CVPR, AAAI, IJCV, TIP, TDSC, and BMVC, and I received the Best Student Paper Runner-Up Award at BMVC 2020.
